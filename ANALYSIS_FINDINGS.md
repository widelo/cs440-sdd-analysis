# SpecMine SDD analysis findings

Generated by `python -m src.pipeline` from the unchanged cleaned CSVs. All findings describe this sample and first detected specification commits, a proxy for adoption. They do not establish causation or GitHub-wide prevalence.

## Data and validation

The sample has **73,031 distinct repositories** and **75,866 repository-category pairs**, with 21 category labels. Repository keys and pair keys are unique. Metadata, category counts, specification totals, first dates, status flags and timestamp chronology pass validation. DATA_COLUMNS.md was read completely and its column definitions and age-filter rules were used.

## Limitations and interpretation

- **Sampling and detection:** This is a sample of repositories with detected SDD specifications, with no comparison population or GitHub-wide denominator. Repository inclusion, crawling and detection may vary by time, language and category. Trends are counts of first detections, not active use, true adoption rates, retention, or causal effects.
- **Time coverage:** Detected dates run from 2024-01-02 to 2026-07-11. The dataset ends in July 2026. July and 2026 are partial and are not annualized. The latest detected commit is not a verified extraction timestamp; completeness even within earlier calendar periods cannot be assumed.
- **Predating histories:** Imported or migrated commit histories can predate repository creation. Their cause is not established by these tables. They remain in non-age summaries and dated trends, but not age calculations. `results/monthly_repository_counts_by_age_status.csv` supports a sensitivity comparison.
- **Missingness:** 1,583 repositories lack a main language; they are shown separately in the language figure. 187 repositories and 198 category pairs lack first-detection dates and cannot contribute to trends. All other appropriate analyses retain them.
- **Fork distinction:** repo_fork is uniformly False/0, so no fork-vs-non-fork comparison is possible. repo_forks measures the number of forks received and is analyzed separately.
- **Category semantics:** root_bare, docs_embedded and other generic or location labels are detection categories, not named software products. All labels are shown as dataset categories; product identities are not inferred. Category memberships overlap and category counts must not be summed into unique repository counts.
- **Measurement:** Main language is one GitHub label rather than a full language inventory. Stars and forks are snapshot counts. Specification counts cover detected files, and logged-in specification contributors count unique GitHub accounts committing to those files, not all contributors or people. Zero logged-in contributors does not imply no human activity.
- **Conditional ages:** Age results exclude invalid chronology and missing dates; groups may differ in exclusion rates. Rounded zero-day values can include very early adoption. Group median/IQR comparisons are descriptive, and IQRs are not confidence intervals. Multiple category rows from one repository are not independent observations.

## Suggestions for the final report

1. Lead with the sample design and the distinct repository/category grains. Use the repository profile, valid-age distribution, and monthly first-detection figures as the main RQ evidence.
2. Report medians and IQRs with valid cohort sizes and excluded status counts; include means and maxima to document skew. Explain log1p axes, which retain zero and the full tail.
3. Compare 2024 and 2025 as observed calendar-year sample counts; discuss 2026 only as an incomplete interval. Avoid interpreting the final partial-month fall as a decline. For follow-up, compare equal observed intervals only after coverage is verified.
4. Use the status-separated monthly table and group exclusion counts to assess sensitivity to predating histories and selection into the valid-age cohort. Investigate history migration with additional evidence before attributing a cause.
5. For broader claims, obtain a documented sampling frame, collection cutoff, comparable non-SDD repositories, and detection precision/recall estimates. Do not infer causal effects of SDD on popularity or collaboration.
